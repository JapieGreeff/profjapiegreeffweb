Eerie Halls: Dungeon Crawler Jam 2021

A procedural dungeon crawler where you plunge into the depths to find your lost cat.

The Concept

Luke is looking for his lost cat and finds that the feline has entered a dungeon made of narrow hallways, populated with monsters looking to end his quest early! He has help from an unlikely source, but must use it wisely as every time he does, he feels slightly weaker.

In Eerie Halls, developed for the Dungeon Crawler Jam 2021, you navigate a procedurally generated dungeon in a throwback to the dungeon crawlers of old. The goal? Find your cat and get out alive.

Built in 7 Days

This game was a week-long effort by a team of five. We chronicled our journey on our development blog, tracking our progress from Day 0 ("The Beginning") through to the frantic finish on Day 7.

Mechanics & Controls

Movement is handled via WASD or arrow keys. A unique mechanic allows you to press H to get help guiding you to the exit, at a cost. Combat involves active timing: use the left key to hit the box on the left for damage, the right key (red box) to block attacks, and the back key to escape when the slider hits yellow.

The procedurally generated dungeon is 25x25 tiles, making for a tight, focused experience.

Reflections

Everything worked well in the dungeon, though in the big rush to submit, the volume and difficulty sliders in the options menu didn't quite get hooked up! It was a fantastic team effort, utilizing assets from the Unity Store (AmusedART) alongside our own custom 2D art and systems.
