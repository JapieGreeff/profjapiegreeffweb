The L-Game: Global Game Jam 2018

A digital reimagining of Edward de Bono's classic lateral thinking game, built in 48 hours.

Edward de Bono's Masterpiece

The L-Game is a simple yet deep abstract strategy game invented by Edward de Bono. It is played on a 4x4 grid with two L-shaped pieces and two neutral pieces. Despite its small board and few pieces, there are over 18,000 possible positions, making it a perfect exercise in lateral thinking and strategic positioning. The goal is simple: maneuver your L-piece to block your opponent from moving theirs.

De Bono designed it to be "the simplest possible serious game"—maximizing strategic depth while minimizing rules and complexity.

Meds vs Germs

For the Global Game Jam 2018, I developed a digital version of this classic. The theme of the jam was "Transmission", which I interpreted as a battle for control of a biological system. In my version, players take on the role of either Meds or Germs, battling to occupy the body (the board). The neutral pieces became "antibodies" or blockers that could be strategically moved to transmit control across the grid.

Under the Hood

I used this project as an opportunity to dive into Python and Pygame. It was a solo project completed over the weekend at the Johannesburg jam site. Developing the logic for valid moves, rotations, and antibody placement in a 48-hour window was a fun challenge that pushed my understanding of game state management and 2D rendering in Python.
